# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- New: a coordinates line on the world map. It shows your position and the position of the cursor.
- The line is in the bottom left corner of the map. If the progress box is there, the line goes to the bottom right corner.
- `/fme coords` shows or hides the line.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-14)

- First version in a repository. The earlier name was Map Fog.
- The fog: the world map shows each area that you did not discover as a flat dark shape. `/fme mode art` shows the art of the area, made darker.
- The progress window (`/explore`), in the layout of the Legacy Challenges view: Azeroth and the continents on the left, plates on the right. A zone plate opens to show its areas, with a check mark for each discovered area.
- A zone that you complete while the addon runs gets the date of that day.
- The minimap button: a left click opens the window, a right click sets the fog to on or off.
- The world map button: a left click shows or hides the progress box on the map, a right click opens the window.
- The progress box on the map shows the progress of the zone, the continent, or Azeroth.
- The area data comes from Veilmap by xElementzx (GPL-3.0-or-later), made from the data tables of WoW build 1.60.1.69913.
